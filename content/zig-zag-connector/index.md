In [a previous post](/bending-line/), we created a fancy connector where a straight line bends (or stretches) based on the distance between the connected elements. This time, let's try a foldable zig-zag connector. 

{% image "./image.png", "A CSS-only zig-zag connector" %}

Drag the circles, but be careful; the connector isn't stretchy and can break. Don't pull the circles too far!
